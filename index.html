OpsPilot: an AI operations agent where the LLM proposes and policy decides

Most agent demos let the model act directly. In finance and operations that is not acceptable, so OpsPilot runs on one rule: the model may only propose a plan - deterministic policy decides what runs.

  • LLM planner (Gemini / OpenAI / Claude) validated against a plan contract, with a deterministic fallback
  • Human approval for risky steps and prompt-injection screening (OWASP LLM01)
  • Idempotency keys - the same payment can never be prepared twice
  • Audit ledger, Prometheus metrics, OpenTelemetry-style traces - 354 automated tests in CI

💼7 Nov 2026

Agentic Finance Crew: can you trust a multi-agent system with spend approvals?

Three agents review every expense - intake, policy analyst, approver - but a tested rule engine makes the final call.

  • MCP server: Claude, ChatGPT or Copilot can submit requests and read decisions, but can never approve
  • Prompt-injection + PII guard - anything suspicious goes to a human
  • Model card + per-decision explanation records for auditors
  • 1,000-case benchmark: 100% accuracy, 0 false approvals - 209 tests

🎥7 Nov 2026

AI Video Intelligence: hours of store CCTV turned into a short, risk-ranked report

Loss-prevention teams cannot watch every hour of footage. This pipeline does the first pass offline - no footage leaves the site - and hands people only what matters.

  • CCTV clip → camera health → detection / tracking / zones → POS-exception risk scoring → human review
  • Register, POS-void and floor incidents ranked by risk, with evidence frames and timestamps
  • 161 automated tests in CI; the demo uses 100% synthetic data
